Privacy policy

shoprite-staples. Last updated October 9, 2026.

What it accesses

Read-only access to the owner's Gmail (the gmail.readonly scope), used only to find order emails sent by ShopRite. It cannot send, change or delete email.

What it does with it

It extracts the items, quantities and pickup dates from those order emails and stores them on the owner's own computer and home server. Nothing is uploaded to any other service, shared, or sold, and no other email content is kept.

Who uses it

Only the owner. The app is not offered to anyone else.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local data files removes everything the app stored.
